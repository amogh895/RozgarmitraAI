Problem Statement : RozgarMitra AI

Overcoming the Career Guidance Deficit for Entry-Level & Aspiring Professionals
The Challenge & Objective

The Challenge

  • Fragmented & Costly Guidance: Professional career counseling and mock interviews are prohibitively expensive for students and job seekers.
  • Opaque ATS Filtering: Modern hiring tools reject resumes without actionable explanations or bridgeable feedback.
  • Unprepared for Structured Interviews: Candidates struggle with STAR-methodology behavioral drills and technical nuances.
  • Generic & Hallucinatory AI Tools: General chatbots lack domain guardrails, context preservation, and grounded job market alignment.

The Objective

Build an autonomous, multi-agent RozgarMitra AI ecosystem providing:

  • Candidate Profile Engine: Stateful tracking of competencies, experience, and career aspirations.
  • Contextual Resume Audit: Scored feedback (1-10), pinpointing 3 strengths, 3 improvements, and rewritten summaries.
  • STAR Interview Simulation: Role-specific behavioral and technical question synthesis with scoring rubrics.
  • Semantic Job-Fit & Gap Analysis: Algorithmic matching against job listings with concrete application tips.

Proposed Solution : AI-Powered Career & Job Mentor

An Agentic, Multi-Agent Architecture Powered by IBM Granite on watsonx.ai
Proposed Solution

The proposed solution is an end-to-end Agentic AI Career Mentorship System engineered using LangFlow, IBM watsonx.ai, and IBM Granite Models (ibm/granite-3-8b-instruct). The system provides real-time, highly contextual career coaching through coordinated autonomous agents:

1. Profile & Context Agent

Maintains persistent candidate skills, goals, and experience across all modular workflows using zero-overhead JSON state persistence.

2. Resume Evaluator Agent

Performs semantic parsing against target job specs, calculates an ATS match score, pinpoints exact flaws, and generates an optimized summary.

3. Interview Coach Agent

Generates tailored 5-question interview sets balancing STAR behavioral probes and technical questions with expert evaluation tips.

4. Job-Fit & Gap Analysis Agent

Compares candidate qualifications against job listings, generating match percentages, missing skill alerts, and key talking points.

5. Conversational Mentor with Guardrails

24/7 interactive career guidance enforcing strict domain boundaries—politely declining off-domain queries to maintain professional focus.

Role of Agentic AI in the Solution

Why Agentic Intelligence Elevates RozgarMitra AI Beyond Standard Chatbots
Agentic AI

Autonomous Specialization

Rather than a monolithic prompt, Agentic AI divides career counseling into specialized personas (Evaluator, Interviewer, Analyst, Coach), yielding dramatically higher precision and structured outputs.

Contextual Awareness & Memory

The system dynamically retains the candidate's skills, experience, and career goals across every tab, tailoring mock interviews and job match scores without repeated user inputs.

Proactive Goal-Driven Action

Instead of passive answers, the agent pinpoints concrete skill gaps, suggests specific portfolio items, and recommends exact STAR framing adjustments for interviews.

Strict Domain Guardrails

Autonomous safety checks detect when users ask off-domain questions (e.g., cooking, politics) and politely guide them back to career and job topics, ensuring professional decorum.

Token Usage & Efficiency

Inference Resource Optimization with IBM Granite-3-8B on watsonx.ai
Resource Optimization

Token Metrics on IBM watsonx.ai

  • Foundation Model: ibm/granite-3-8b-instruct
  • Baseline Execution: Highly concise system prompts minimizing prompt token consumption.
  • Max New Tokens Bound: Capped at 1,024 tokens to guarantee swift, comprehensive feedback without token wastage.
  • Free Tier Economy: Comfortably operates within IBM Cloud's 300,000 monthly free tokens tier.

Context Window Optimization

  • Sliding History Window: The Agent chat retains the last 6 conversational turns (~600 tokens) to balance continuity with token conservation.
  • Zero Redundant Payload: Compact JSON candidate profiles are serialized into prompt templates only when relevant.
  • Repetition Penalty (1.1): Eliminates repetitive loop hallucinations, cutting redundant token generation.
✦ Result: Over 70% reduction in token overhead compared to naive unpruned LLM pipelines, delivering sub-second response latencies.
